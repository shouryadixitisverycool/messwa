1. Mess API key

Your key for the IIIT mess portal. messwa uses it to read your own registrations and QR. It's stored encrypted and never shown again. messwa checks it with the portal before saving, so you need to be on the IIIT network.

The server has no MESSWA_SECRET_KEY, so it can't store keys yet. Ask an admin to set it.

2. WhatsApp

Link your WhatsApp as a linked device. messwa reads the mess groups with it, and later sells your meals from it when you ask. Your chats, buyers and sales stay private to you. Linking sends no messages.

5. WhatsApp Pay

Buyers pay you with WhatsApp Pay, and messwa sends the QR once the payment shows up in the chat. It can't see GPay or other UPI apps. Turn WhatsApp Pay on for the number you linked:

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to Payments: under Settings on iPhone, in the ⋮ menu on Android.
  2. Tap Add payment method and pick your bank.
  3. Verify your number and set or confirm your UPI PIN.
